FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Everything stays on your device. No account needed — your photos and item data are never sent to an external server. Image analysis and similarity search run entirely on-device. If you want multiple devices, Pro’s opt-in iCloud sync keeps them in step through your own iCloud.

What happens when I switch devices?

Pro gives you two ways: turn on iCloud sync and just sign in on the new device, or export your items and photos as a single ZIP file and import it — no cloud required.

Do sold items disappear?

No. They're simply marked as sold and stay in your records. You can toggle their visibility, which makes the app handy as a sales log too.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad, with the Mac version coming soon. With Pro’s iCloud sync, every device opens the same collection.
